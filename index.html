About

Hey, I'm Dillian! 👋

I'm a technology enthusiast, lifelong tinkerer, and former operations manager with a knack for figuring out how things work. Whether I'm writing code, building something from scratch, or taking apart a perfectly functioning piece of technology just to see what's inside, I'm usually working on something.

I spent nearly a decade at Menards working in operations management, logistics, and construction sales, where I learned a lot about leading teams, solving problems, and keeping things running when the day inevitably doesn't go according to plan.

Somewhere along the way, my interest in technology turned into something much bigger. I graduated from The Software Guild's full-stack Java bootcamp in 2022, and I'm currently pursuing my bachelor's degree in Computer Science at Western Governors University with anticiapted graduation in September 2027.

While I enjoy programming, my interests go well beyond what's happening on a computer screen. I'm fascinated by network infrastructure/servers, small electronics, automation, smart-home technology, and the systems that keep everything connected and running. There's something incredibly satisfying about understanding how all the pieces fit together.

These days, I'm looking to bring my operations management experience and technical education together in a career where I can continue learning, solve interesting problems, and get my hands on some cool technology.

And when I'm not doing any of that? There's a good chance I'm spending time with my family, working on another project around the house, or convincing myself I definitely need one more gadget.

Thanks for stopping by!
